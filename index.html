About

I lead engineering where data and AI meet: building the systems that show which AI tools actually work, what they cost, and who is accountable when they act on someone's behalf. My focus is AI governance that is enforced in code, not just written in policy.

What I lead

  • AI governance programsAI system inventories, risk assessment, and controls mapped to the NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act, including controls for AI agents.
  • AI measurement and evaluationTelemetry and metrics for internal AI tools: adoption, task success, time saved and engineering ROI.
  • Data governance and compliancePrivacy, consent and regulatory requirements built into how data is collected and used, across engineering teams and vendors.
  • Data platforms and measurement infrastructureInstrumentation and pipelines for large-scale product telemetry.
  • AI agent identity and authorizationLeast-privilege access for agents, consent versus authorization, and controls enforced outside the model.
  • Engineering leadershipLeading a team of 10 engineers and engineering managers.
